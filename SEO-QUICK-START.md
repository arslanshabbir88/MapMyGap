## 🎯 Do These 5 Things in Next 30 Minutes

### 1. Set Up Google Search Console (10 min)
**Link:** https://search.google.com/search-console

**Steps:**
1. Click "Start now"
2. Add property: `mapmygap.com`
3. Verify ownership (use "HTML tag" method):
   - Copy the meta tag they give you
   - Add it to your `index.html` (I can help with this)
4. Click "Submit sitemap" → Enter: `https://mapmygap.com/sitemap.xml`
5. Done! Google will start indexing your site

**Why:** This tells Google your site exists and helps you see search performance.

---

### 2. Install Microsoft Clarity (5 min) - FREE!
**Link:** https://clarity.microsoft.com

**Steps:**
1. Sign up (free forever)
2. Click "Add new project"
3. Enter: MapMyGap
4. Copy the tracking code
5. Add it to your `index.html` (I can help)

**Why:** See heatmaps, session recordings, and user behavior. Understand what users click and where they get stuck.

## 💡 Pro Tips for Maximum Impact

### **Reddit:**
- ✅ Be helpful, not salesy
- ✅ Share your story ("I built this because...")
- ✅ Ask for feedback
- ✅ Respond to every comment within 1 hour
- ❌ Don't spam multiple subreddits at once

### **Hacker News:**
- ✅ Technical details matter (share your stack)
- ✅ Be transparent about challenges
- ✅ Respond thoughtfully to criticism
- ✅ Share interesting technical decisions
- ❌ Don't be defensive

### **LinkedIn:**
- ✅ Share your founder journey
- ✅ Include specific metrics
- ✅ Use 5-8 relevant hashtags
- ✅ Post 7-9am EST or 12-1pm EST
- ❌ Don't use "salesy" language

### **Twitter:**
- ✅ Use threads (10+ tweets)
- ✅ Include visuals (screenshots/GIFs)
- ✅ Share metrics openly
- ✅ Engage with replies
- ❌ Don't just post links

## 🚨 Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. ❌ **Not responding fast enough** → Respond within 1 hour
2. ❌ **Being defensive about criticism** → Thank them, take notes
3. ❌ **Posting and ghosting** → Engage all day
4. ❌ **Posting at wrong times** → 8-11am EST, Tuesday-Thursday
5. ❌ **Not having demo video** → Make one with Loom (5 min)
6. ❌ **Spamming too many places** → Quality over quantity
7. ❌ **Not tracking metrics** → Watch Google Analytics
8. ❌ **Giving up after Day 1** → Keep posting for 2 weeks
